# v0.7 Comparative Performance Intelligence / Day Lab

MorningstarModbusFrontend v0.7 adds a comparative operating-day workspace at `/site/day-lab`.

## Goal

Day Lab answers a different question from the live dashboards and Site Replay: **how did this day differ from the site's own recent operating history?**

It compares source-backed normalized history without moving anomaly detection or diagnosis into the browser.

## Comparison modes

Operators can select any recorded day and compare it with:

- the previous day;
- the prior 7-day median;
- the prior 30-day median.

When the selected day is today, all comparison days are truncated to the same local-time progress so a partial current day is never compared with a complete historical day.

## Metrics

Day Lab uses existing 15-minute normalized system history for:

- `solar_input_power_w`;
- `charge_output_power_w`;
- `battery_voltage_v`;
- `battery_charge_current_a`;
- `daily_charge_wh`.

It derives and clearly labels:

- integrated solar-input energy from available 15-minute PV power buckets;
- integrated charge-output energy from available 15-minute output-power buckets;
- peak solar input;
- peak charge output;
- maximum battery voltage;
- average charge current;
- telemetry coverage.

The controller daily-charge counter remains a separate comparison row rather than being merged with locally integrated power history.

## Historical curves

The main chart normalizes each day onto local time-of-day and overlays:

- the selected day;
- the selected comparison baseline;
- the prior 30-day median curve.

No missing buckets are interpolated.

## Evidence observations

Day Lab reports factual deltas such as "solar input energy was 14% below the comparison". These are comparative observations, not diagnoses. The browser does not claim that an incident, weather condition, battery state, or communications event caused a difference.

System events and persistent incidents that overlap the selected day are shown as correlated evidence with direct links into Site Replay around the evidence timestamp.

## Relationship to Site Replay

The intended investigation path is:

1. notice a measurable day-over-day or baseline divergence in Day Lab;
2. inspect correlated incidents and events;
3. open Site Replay around the relevant timestamp;
4. inspect synchronized historical telemetry and incident context;
5. return to the live Digital Twin when current structural context is needed.

## Backend contract

v0.7 uses existing read-only APIs:

- `GET /v1/systems/{system_uid}/history` with 15-minute bucketed history;
- `GET /v1/systems/{system_uid}/events`;
- `GET /v1/systems/{system_uid}/incidents`.

The bucketed history path added in MorningstarModbusAPI makes the 30-day normalized comparison window practical even with high-granularity raw capture.

## Safety and semantics

Day Lab performs no controller writes, configuration changes, alarm acknowledgements, SOC inference, missing-data interpolation, or causal diagnosis. Historical medians use prior days only. Integrated energy is coverage-aware and remains distinct from controller-native daily energy counters.
