# v0.8 Operator Answers / Site Questions

MorningstarModbusFrontend v0.8 adds a question-driven operations workspace at `/site/questions`.

## Goal

The existing frontend exposes rich evidence through Site Overview, Forecast, Digital Twin, Replay, Day Lab, Operations Intelligence, Power Flow, Energy, History, Events, Topology, and controller workspaces. Operators should not need to know which page or API resource answers a common operational question.

Operator Answers turns that existing evidence into a transparent catalog of direct questions and concise answers.

## Initial question catalog

v0.8 ships with 28 canonical questions across six categories:

- **Now** — controller availability, current solar input, battery voltage, controller charge current, and charge stage.
- **Forecast** — observed solar input energy, remaining solar, end-of-day projection, progress versus historical P50, uncertainty, model accuracy, Float probability/time, evidence depth, and offline/weather provenance.
- **Performance** — current solar versus local baseline, matched-time 7-day solar and charge comparisons, input/output divergence, peak solar, and battery-voltage comparison.
- **Health** — site health score, active incidents, faults, and alarms.
- **Data quality** — telemetry coverage and the distinction between conflicts, unsupported metrics, and temporarily missing observations.
- **Investigation** — highest-priority current evidence and a deterministic recommendation for what to inspect next.

## Search and navigation

The page provides:

- full-text filtering over questions, answers, categories, and keywords;
- category chips;
- an answerable-now count;
- explicit Unknown states when evidence is missing;
- evidence disclosures on each answer;
- direct links to the source workspace that proves or investigates the answer.

Evidence links target existing pages such as:

- Solar Day Planner;
- Day Lab;
- Site Replay;
- Operations Intelligence;
- Power Flow;
- Site History;
- Controllers.

When an active incident is the highest-priority finding, the answer can deep-link directly into Site Replay around the incident opening time.

## Evidence model

Operator Answers is deliberately **not** a browser-side LLM diagnosis system.

The answer builder is a pure, testable synthesis layer over existing backend evidence:

- normalized current system metrics;
- system health and controller counts;
- decomposable health score;
- persistent incidents;
- local P10/P50/P90 solar baseline;
- offline predictive forecast and forecast backtesting;
- matched-time 15-minute Day Lab history;
- capability-aware metric availability semantics.

It does not interpolate missing history, infer SOC from battery voltage, convert controller charge current into battery net current, or promote correlation into causation.

## Comparative questions

For current-day comparisons, v0.8 reuses the Day Lab rules:

- prior seven days only;
- comparison through the same local-time progress as the current day;
- 15-minute normalized history;
- no interpolation through missing buckets;
- telemetry coverage surfaced with the answer.

Solar-input and charge-output energy remain locally integrated power-history quantities and are not relabeled as controller daily charge counters.

## Question examples

The workspace can directly answer questions such as:

- How is the site doing right now?
- Are all controllers online?
- What needs attention first?
- Is solar input normal for this time of day?
- How much solar power is arriving right now?
- How much solar input energy have we observed today?
- How much solar is likely left today?
- What is projected total solar input by end of day?
- Are we ahead of or behind the normal solar trajectory?
- How uncertain is today's forecast?
- How accurate has the local forecast model been?
- Will the controllers likely reach Float today?
- When should all controllers reach Float?
- Is today producing more or less solar than the recent 7-day median?
- Are solar input and charge output moving together versus recent history?
- Is telemetry coverage good enough to trust today's comparison?
- Are key measurements conflicting, unsupported, or temporarily missing?
- What should I inspect next?

## Safety and read-only boundary

v0.8 does not introduce Modbus writes, configuration changes, alarm acknowledgements, automation, or browser-side control. It is a presentation and evidence-synthesis feature only.
